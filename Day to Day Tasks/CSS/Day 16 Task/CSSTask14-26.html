About This Hotel

Set in a modern building with a glass facade, this warm hotel on a main road is a 13-minute walk from Chepauk train station and 19 minutes' walk from Marina Beach.

The unassuming rooms have free Wi-Fi and flat-screen TVs, plus safes, and tea and coffeemaking facilities. Upgraded rooms add sofas and minifridges. Room service is available.

There's a low-key restaurant. Parking is offered.

Tea Recipe

Ingredients

  • 1 cup Water
  • 1 cup Milk
  • 2 teaspoons Tea Powder
  • 2 teaspoons Sugar

Steps

  1. Boil the water.
  2. Add the tea powder.
  3. Add the milk and sugar.
  4. Boil for 2 minutes.
  5. Filter and serve hot.

Important:Use fresh milk and clean drinking water.
Preparation time: 10 minutes.

Biriyani Recipe:

Ingredients:

  • Chicken: 500g cut into pieces
  • Basmati Rice: 2 cups (soaked for 30 minutes)
  • Yogurt: 1/2 cup plain curd
  • Onions: 3 large sliced thin (fried crisp for garnish)
  • Ginger-Garlic Paste: 1.5 tablespoons
  • Whole Spices: Bay leaves, green cardamom, cloves, cinnamon, and cumin
  • Powdered Spices: Red chili powder, turmeric, and garam masala
  • Fresh Herbs: Chopped mint and coriander leaves
  • Garnish: Saffron soaked in warm milk and 2 tbsp ghee

Important: Use a fresh chicken dont use frozen chicken and use the fresh herbs: Chopped mint and
coriander leaves

Steps:

  1. Marinate the chicken
  2. Fry the onions
  3. Cook the chicken base
  4. Boil the rice
  5. Layer the biryani
  6. Layer the biryani
  7. Dum cooking
  8. Dum cooking
